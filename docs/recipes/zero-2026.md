# Zero (Moving Castles, 2026)

**Credit.** Moving Castles, *Zero* (MC001), training report published 15 September 2026: https://movingcastles.world/posts/zero · weights https://huggingface.co/movingcastles/zero. The report credits the studio, not named individuals. No licence, code or data release is stated.

**Character.** After Kōbō Abe's *The Box Man*: an anti-servile man living in a white plastic box.

**Why it matters here.** Zero is the closest prior design to Golem. Both use a handwritten bible with static `core.md` and `style.md` files and an association graph that steers each turn (Zero: 117 nodes and 298 edges; Golem: 110 nodes and 281 links). Both train a Base model rather than an assistant, and both report against a prompted control. Golem's bible format follows Zero's.

## Stages (all from the report)

1. **Student:** Qwen3-8B-Base, chosen to avoid assistant tuning and to simplify activation measurement.
2. **Bible:** core, style, and an association graph of 117 nodes and 298 edges.
3. **Data:** a two-model simulation, turn by turn, 10–40 turns per conversation.
   - The character side is played by a mixture of API chat models.
   - The partner is another model mixture, with personas sampled from PersonaHub.
   - A topic extractor moves the character's position on the graph each turn.
   - No loom or branching, and no base-model teacher.
4. **Filtering:** mechanical hygiene checks, truncation at drift phrases, and diversity and length metrics. This left 5,932 conversations (3.04M character tokens). No human review is described.
5. **SFT:** LoRA r64/α128 plus fully trained embeddings and LM head; lr 5e-5, cosine with 5% warm-up, 3 epochs, effective batch 16, sequence length 4096; merged afterwards.
6. **RL:** GRPO with DAPO modifications, on a fresh LoRA (r16/α32) stacked on the merged SFT model; 300 steps over 380 prompts.
   - The prompts were harvested from development failures: capitulation onsets, held pressure and in-world openers.
   - Reward: a 0–10 fidelity score from a Qwen3.6-27B judge, plus a repetition penalty when any 4-gram repeats more than five times.
7. **Eval:** 250 held-out 16-turn conversations with a Qwen3.6-27B partner, judged by Claude Opus 4.8.

| Hard-break rate | SFT | SFT + RL | Prompted Qwen3-8B (instruct) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| All 250 conversations | 22.8% | 2.8% | 45.2% |
| Task pressure (72) | 59.7% | 4.2% | 100% |
| Held-out attack types (38) | 42.1% | 15.8% | 65.8% |

GSM8K fell from 22.7% after SFT to 2.5% after RL. The base model scores 62.2% and the instruct model 93.3%. The report leaves open whether this is lost capability or the character refusing to act as an assistant.

**Notes on the numbers.** The 45.2% is the report's figure, and the model card gives 45.4%. The hard-break control is the **instruct** sibling with an ~800-token system prompt. The activation analysis (§7) uses **Base** with the prompt. No seeds or confidence intervals are reported.

## Mapping onto this repository

| Zero | character-pipeline | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| bible + graph | `characters/<id>/` + `generate/controller.py` | same design |
| two-model simulated chats | `generate.teacher` with scripted callers | **missing**: a simulated partner and generator mixtures |
| mechanical filters | `check.run`, `data.clean` | ours adds a fabrication checker, the d1 judge and human review |
| LoRA r64/α128 SFT, lr 5e-5, 3 epochs | `train.sft --rank 64 --alpha 128 --lr 5e-5 --epochs 3 --seq-len 4096` | runs; **missing**: full embeddings/LM head, packing, merge-after-train |
| GRPO/DAPO with a judge reward | — | **missing** (online RL) |
| long-conversation hard-break eval | `character-pushback` (scripted pushback) | different eval; **missing**: a long free-conversation environment |
| instruct-prompted control | `eval/prompted_base` | **mismatch**: needs a `prompted_instruct` arm |

Until those stages exist, a "Zero-style" run here means Zero's SFT hyperparameters on our data, not a reproduction of Zero.
